SPACESIFT

What can telescopes find, and what do they miss?

SpaceSift is an open research project about one question: when astronomers search the sky for something, how often do they actually catch it, and how often are they fooled? It looks at that question in two places: asteroids that could come near Earth, and planets orbiting other stars.

WHAT IT IS

Two free tools. A live dashboard that ranks newly spotted asteroids by how likely they are to be real near-Earth objects. And a lab that tests how well planet-hunting software finds planets, including the ones it misses.

WHO IT'S FOR

Observers deciding where to point a telescope tonight. Students and researchers who want to see how detection works, not just the headline results. Anyone curious how we know what's out there.

WHY IT MATTERS

Telescope time is scarce. A new asteroid that isn't followed up within days can be lost, and finding the dangerous ones early is the core of planetary defense. For planets, a missed planet looks exactly like no planet, so any estimate of how common planets are depends on knowing what the searches miss.

NEOCP vetting

Which newly spotted asteroid candidates are real near-Earth objects? A machine-learned ranking of every object awaiting confirmation, so follow-up telescopes chase the ones that matter.

  • Live sky map of current candidates
  • Real-NEO probability for each object
  • Model trained on every past candidate with a known outcome

Why it matters: most candidates turn out to be ordinary asteroids. Ranking them helps the few that could approach Earth get confirmed before they slip away.

INTERACTIVE · EXPERIMENTS

Transit Lab

Where does planet-hunting break? Plant a planet in a star's brightness record and see whether the search finds it, then explore experiments on tens of thousands of planted planets.

  • Try it in your browser: simulated or real Kepler stars
  • Finding: stellar pulsations can hide planets completely
  • Every experiment reproducible from its recorded code and seed

Why it matters: estimates of how common Earth-like planets are have to correct for planets the search missed. This lab measures those misses directly.
